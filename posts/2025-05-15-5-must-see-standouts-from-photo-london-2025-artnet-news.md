# 5 Must-See Standouts from Photo London 2025 - Artnet News

Photo London is an annual event that showcases the best in contemporary photography from around the world. This year's edition, held in London, featured a diverse range of works from both established and emerging artists. In this blog post, we will highlight five standout photographers whose work caught our attention at Photo London 2025, as reported by Artnet News.

## Introduction

Photo London is a must-see event for photographers looking to be inspired by the latest trends and innovations in the world of photography. With hundreds of galleries and artists showcasing their work, it can be overwhelming to navigate the vast array of images on display. That's why we've curated a list of five must-see standouts to help you make the most of your visit to Photo London 2025.

### 1. **Kwame Brathwaite**

Kwame Brathwaite's work focuses on capturing the beauty and strength of the Black community. His powerful images challenge stereotypes and celebrate the diversity of Black culture. One of his standout pieces at Photo London 2025 was a series of portraits that captured the essence of Black joy and resilience. ![Kwame Brathwaite Image](/path/to/kwame_brathwaite_image.jpg)

### 2. **Hannah Starkey**

Hannah Starkey is known for her cinematic and emotive portraits of women. Her work at Photo London 2025 explored themes of femininity, identity, and the everyday struggles faced by women. Starkey's use of light and composition creates a sense of intimacy and vulnerability in her photographs, making them truly captivating to behold.

### 3. **Hans Eijkelboom**

Hans Eijkelboom's work blurs the lines between art and documentary photography. His series of street photographs at Photo London 2025 captured the essence of everyday life in a unique and compelling way. Eijkelboom's keen eye for detail and his ability to find beauty in the mundane make his work a must-see for any photography enthusiast.

### 4. **Rinko Kawauchi**

Rinko Kawauchi's ethereal and poetic images at Photo London 2025 transport viewers to a dreamlike world. Her use of color and light creates a sense of serenity and tranquility in her photographs, inviting viewers to pause and reflect on the beauty of the natural world. Kawauchi's work is a reminder of the power of photography to evoke emotion and inspire contemplation.

### 5. **Cindy Sherman**

Cindy Sherman is a pioneer in the world of self-portraiture, using photography to explore themes of identity, gender, and representation. Her work at Photo London 2025 continued to push boundaries and challenge conventions, showcasing the power of photography as a tool for self-expression and social commentary. Sherman's bold and provocative images are sure to leave a lasting impression on anyone who views them.

## Practical Tips for Visiting Photo London 2025

- **Plan Your Visit:** With so much to see at Photo London, it's important to plan your visit in advance. Make a list of the photographers and galleries you want to see, and prioritize your time accordingly.
- **Bring a Notebook:** Take notes on the photographers and works that inspire you. This will help you remember them later and can serve as a valuable reference for future projects.
- **Engage with the Artists:** Don't be afraid to strike up a conversation with the artists exhibiting their work at Photo London. They can provide valuable insights into their creative process and inspiration.
- **Take Breaks:** Photo London can be overwhelming, so be sure to take breaks and give yourself time to rest and recharge. This will help you appreciate the art on display and prevent burnout.
- **Capture the Moment:** Don't forget to bring your camera or smartphone to capture the beauty of Photo London for yourself. You never know when inspiration will strike, so be ready to seize the moment.

## Conclusion

Photo London 2025 was a showcase of the best and brightest in contemporary photography, with standout works that captivated and inspired visitors. From the powerful portraits of Kwame Brathwaite to the ethereal images of Rinko Kawauchi, each photographer brought a unique perspective to the event. Whether you're a seasoned photographer or a budding enthusiast, Photo London is a must-see event that offers something for everyone. So mark your calendars for next year's edition and get ready to be amazed by the world of photography in all its glory.
