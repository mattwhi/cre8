# Binstax: Using a Fuji Instax Square Adapter for Mamiya RB67

In the world of photography, the Mamiya RB67 is a beloved medium format camera known for its exceptional image quality and versatility. However, one of the limitations of this camera is the lack of instant film options. But fear not, as there is a solution that allows you to shoot instant film with your Mamiya RB67 - the Binstax adapter.

## Introduction to Binstax

The Binstax adapter is a clever accessory that allows you to use Fuji Instax Square film with your Mamiya RB67. This means you can enjoy the convenience and instant gratification of instant film photography with the stunning image quality of the Mamiya RB67.

## Practical Tips for Using Binstax with Mamiya RB67

Here are some practical tips for using the Binstax adapter with your Mamiya RB67:

1. **Installation**: Installing the Binstax adapter is easy. Simply attach it to the back of your Mamiya RB67 in place of the standard film back.

2. **Film Loading**: Loading the Fuji Instax Square film into the adapter is straightforward. Make sure to follow the instructions that come with the adapter to ensure proper loading.

3. **Exposure Settings**: Adjust your exposure settings on the Mamiya RB67 as you would with regular film. Keep in mind that instant film has different characteristics than traditional film, so it may take some trial and error to get the exposure just right.

4. **Focusing**: The Mamiya RB67 is a manual focus camera, so be sure to take your time to ensure your subject is in focus before taking the shot.

5. **Composition**: Instant film is precious, so take the time to compose your shot carefully before pressing the shutter button.

6. **Handling**: Instant film is sensitive to light, so be sure to shield the exposed film from direct sunlight as it develops.
