## Introduction to Bokeh

Before we delve into the specifics of TTArtisan’s new cine lens, let’s first understand what bokeh is and why it is an essential element in photography. Bokeh refers to the aesthetic quality of the out-of-focus areas in a photograph, typically the background. A pleasing bokeh can add depth and dimension to an image, drawing the viewer’s attention to the main subject.

![Bokeh](/path/to/bokeh-image.jpg)

2. Play around with different focal lengths to see how they impact the bokeh. A longer focal length will compress the background, making the bokeh more prominent, while a shorter focal length will create a more subtle bokeh effect.
3. Consider the distance between the subject and the background when composing your shot. A larger distance will result in a more pronounced bokeh, while a closer distance will create a more subtle bokeh effect.
