# Behind the Lens with Neyssa Lee Photography: Simple Tips for Taking Photos of Your Graduate

Graduation season is upon us, and as a photographer, capturing this milestone moment for families is a joyous privilege. Whether you are a professional photographer or a parent looking to snap some memorable shots of your graduate, there are some simple tips and tricks you can use to ensure that your photos turn out beautifully. To help you make the most of this special occasion, we reached out to the talented Neyssa Lee Photography for some expert advice on how to capture the perfect graduation photos.

![Graduation photo](/path/to/graduation-photo.jpg)

## Introduction

Neyssa Lee is an award-winning photographer based in Snoqualmie, Washington, specializing in family and portrait photography. With over a decade of experience in the field, Neyssa has a keen eye for capturing special moments and turning them into timeless memories. We sat down with Neyssa to pick her brain on how to take stunning photos of graduates, whether it's for a professional shoot or a DIY session at home.

## Practical Tips

### 1. Choose the Right Location

When it comes to taking graduation photos, the location can make all the difference. Whether you opt for a picturesque outdoor setting or a more traditional indoor backdrop, make sure the location complements the graduate's personality and style. Outdoor locations such as parks, gardens, or even urban cityscapes can provide a beautiful backdrop for your photos.

### 2. Pay Attention to Lighting

Lighting is crucial in photography, and natural light is often the best choice for capturing stunning images. If you're shooting outdoors, aim for the golden hours of sunrise or sunset when the light is soft and flattering. Avoid harsh midday sun, as it can create unflattering shadows and highlights. If you're shooting indoors, try to position the graduate near a window to take advantage of natural light.

### 3. Use Props and Accessories

Props and accessories can add a personal touch to your graduation photos and help tell a story. Whether it's a cap and gown, a diploma, or a favorite book, incorporating meaningful props can make your photos more interesting and unique. Encourage the graduate to bring items that reflect their interests and achievements to add a personal touch to the photos.

### 4. Capture Candid Moments

While posed shots are important, don't forget to capture candid moments that showcase the graduate's personality and emotions. Encourage them to laugh, smile, and interact naturally with their surroundings to capture authentic moments that will make your photos stand out. Candid shots can often be the most memorable and meaningful.

### 5. Focus on Details

Don't forget to pay attention to the little details that can make a big difference in your photos. From the graduate's outfit and accessories to the background and composition, every element in the frame should be carefully considered. Take the time to adjust angles, framing, and focus to ensure that your photos are visually appealing and well-composed.

### 6. Edit with Care

Editing plays a crucial role in enhancing your graduation photos and giving them a polished look. Use editing software to adjust exposure, colors, and contrast to make your photos pop. However, remember that less is often more when it comes to editing. Avoid over-editing your photos and strive for a natural, timeless look that will stand the test of time.

## Conclusion

Capturing graduation photos is a special opportunity to celebrate a significant milestone in a graduate's life. Whether you're a professional photographer or a parent with a camera, following these simple tips from Neyssa Lee Photography can help you take stunning photos that will be cherished for years to come. Remember to choose the right location, pay attention to lighting, use props and accessories, capture candid moments, focus on details, and edit with care to create beautiful and memorable graduation photos.
