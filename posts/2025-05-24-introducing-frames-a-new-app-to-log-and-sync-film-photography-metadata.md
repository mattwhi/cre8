# Introducing Frames – A New App to Log and Sync Film Photography Metadata

In the world of film photography, keeping track of all the details related to your shots can be a daunting task. From exposure settings to film types, there's a lot of information to remember and organize. That's where Frames comes in – a new app designed specifically for film photographers to easily log and sync metadata for their shots.

## **Introduction**

Frames is a user-friendly app that allows photographers to input and store metadata for each of their film photographs. This includes details such as camera settings, film type, location, and more. With Frames, photographers can easily access this information later on for reference or sharing with others.

One of the key features of Frames is its ability to sync metadata across multiple devices. This means that photographers can input information on their phone while out shooting, and then access it later on their computer for editing or printing. This seamless integration makes it easy to keep track of all the details for each shot, no matter where you are.

## **Practical Tips**

Here are some practical tips for using Frames to log and sync your film photography metadata effectively:

1. **Create Detailed Entries:** When logging metadata for your shots, be as detailed as possible. Include information on exposure settings, film type, lens used, and any other relevant details. This will make it easier to search for specific shots later on.

2. **Use Keywords:** Frames allows you to add keywords to your entries, making it easier to search for specific shots later on. Utilize this feature to categorize your photos and make them easier to find.

3. **Sync Regularly:** Make sure to sync your metadata across all your devices regularly to ensure that you have access to the most up-to-date information. This will help you stay organized and prevent any data loss.

4. **Share with Others:** Frames also allows you to share your metadata with others, making it easy to collaborate on projects or show off your work. Use this feature to connect with other photographers and showcase your unique style.
