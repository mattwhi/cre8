# Canon Announces New RF Telephoto, But You Can’t Get It in the US Just Yet

Attention all photographers! Canon has recently announced the release of a new RF telephoto lens that is sure to take your photography to the next level. The Canon RF 800mm F5.6L IS USM and RF 1200mm F8L IS USM are the latest additions to Canon's lineup of high-quality lenses. These lenses are designed for professionals and enthusiasts who demand the best in image quality and performance.

## Introduction to the Canon RF Telephoto Lenses

The Canon RF 800mm F5.6L IS USM and RF 1200mm F8L IS USM are both super-telephoto lenses that are perfect for capturing distant subjects with incredible detail. These lenses are designed for sports, wildlife, and astrophotography, allowing you to get up close and personal with your subjects without having to physically move closer.

![Canon RF Telephoto Lens](/path/to/image)

One of the standout features of these lenses is their Image Stabilization technology, which helps to reduce camera shake and blur, even when shooting at slow shutter speeds. This is especially important when shooting in low light conditions or when using the lenses at their maximum focal lengths.

## Practical Tips for Using the Canon RF Telephoto Lenses

If you are lucky enough to get your hands on one of these new Canon RF telephoto lenses, here are some practical tips to help you get the most out of them:

1. **Use a sturdy tripod**: When shooting with such long focal lengths, even the slightest camera shake can result in blurry images. Using a sturdy tripod will help to stabilize your camera and ensure sharp images.

2. **Focus carefully**: With such narrow depth of field at longer focal lengths, it is important to focus carefully on your subject to ensure sharpness. Consider using manual focus or focus peaking to ensure precise focus.

3. **Experiment with different apertures**: The wide apertures of these lenses allow for beautiful background blur, but don't be afraid to stop down the aperture for increased depth of field when needed.

4. **Consider using a teleconverter**: If you need even more reach, consider using a teleconverter with these lenses to extend their focal lengths even further.

## Conclusion

While the Canon RF 800mm F5.6L IS USM and RF 1200mm F8L IS USM are not yet available in the US, photographers around the world are eagerly anticipating their release. These lenses promise to deliver outstanding image quality and performance, making them a must-have for any serious photographer.

So keep an eye out for the release of these new Canon RF telephoto lenses and be prepared to take your photography to new heights. With their incredible reach and image quality, these lenses are sure to become a favorite among photographers everywhere.
