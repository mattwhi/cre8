# ZHIYUN Launches New Compact 100W RGB Light For Filmmakers on the Go

In the world of photography and filmmaking, lighting is everything. Whether you're shooting a professional film or capturing a family portrait, having the right lighting can make or break your final product. That's why ZHIYUN, a leading manufacturer of camera stabilization systems, has launched their new compact 100W RGB light designed specifically for filmmakers on the go.

## Introduction
The ZHIYUN compact 100W RGB light is a game-changer for filmmakers who need versatile and portable lighting solutions. With its compact size and powerful output, this light is perfect for on-the-go shooting scenarios where traditional lighting setups are not feasible. Whether you're shooting a documentary in the wilderness or a music video in a dimly lit club, this light has you covered.

## Features
- **RGB Color Control**: With the ability to adjust the color temperature from 2500K to 8500K, as well as full RGB color control, you have endless creative possibilities at your fingertips.
- **Wireless Control**: The light can be controlled remotely via a smartphone app, allowing you to make adjustments without having to physically touch the light.
- **Compact and Lightweight**: Weighing only 500g and measuring just 7.5 inches in length, this light is easy to pack and carry with you wherever you go.
- **High CRI**: With a high CRI rating of 95+, this light ensures accurate color reproduction for natural-looking skin tones and vibrant colors.
- **Built-in Effects**: The light comes with a variety of built-in lighting effects, such as police car, fire, and lightning, to add dynamic lighting to your shots.

## Practical Tips for Using the ZHIYUN RGB Light
1. **Experiment with Different Colors**: Don't be afraid to get creative with the RGB color control feature. Try mixing different colors to create unique lighting effects that enhance the mood of your scene.
2. **Use Diffusion**: To soften the light and avoid harsh shadows, consider using a diffusion panel or a softbox with the light.
3. **Adjust the Intensity**: Depending on the desired look of your shot, you can adjust the intensity of the light to create subtle or dramatic lighting effects.
4. **Combine with Other Lights**: The ZHIYUN RGB light can be used in conjunction with other lights to create a layered lighting setup that adds depth and dimension to your shots.
