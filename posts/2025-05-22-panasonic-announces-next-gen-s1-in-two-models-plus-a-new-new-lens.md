The Panasonic S1 has long been a favorite among photographers for its exceptional image quality and robust build. With the release of the next generation S1, Panasonic has taken things to a whole new level. The new S1 comes in two models: the S1R, which boasts a whopping 47.3 megapixels, and the S1, which features a more modest 24.2 megapixels. In addition to the new cameras, Panasonic has also announced a new lens that is sure to be a game changer for photographers.
