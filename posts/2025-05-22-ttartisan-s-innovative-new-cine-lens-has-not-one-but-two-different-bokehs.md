## Introduction to Bokeh

Bokeh is a term used in photography to describe the out-of-focus areas in an image. It refers to the quality of the blur produced by the lens when shooting at wide apertures. Bokeh can add depth and visual interest to a photo, making the subject stand out against a soft, dreamy background.

Most lenses produce a standard circular bokeh, which is pleasing to the eye and commonly used in portrait photography. However, TTArtisan’s new cine lens takes bokeh to the next level by offering not one, but two different bokeh shapes.

## The Dual Bokeh Feature

The dual bokeh feature of TTArtisan’s cine lens allows photographers to switch between two different bokeh shapes with the twist of a dial. This innovative design gives photographers the freedom to experiment with different looks and styles without having to change lenses or manipulate the image in post-production.

One bokeh shape is a classic circular design, perfect for creating a soft and romantic background for portraits. The other bokeh shape is more angular and geometric, adding a modern and edgy feel to the image. By being able to switch between these two bokeh shapes, photographers can easily customize the look and feel of their photos to suit their personal style.
