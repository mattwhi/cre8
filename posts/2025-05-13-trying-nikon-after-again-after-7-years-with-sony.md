# Trying Nikon Again After 7 Years With Sony

As a photographer, the choice of camera gear is crucial to achieving the desired results in your work. For the past seven years, I have been a loyal Sony user, but recently I decided to give Nikon another try. In this blog post, I will share my experience of switching back to Nikon after so many years with Sony, as well as some practical tips for photographers who are considering making a similar change.

## Introduction

When I first started out in photography, I chose Nikon as my brand of choice. I loved the color rendition, dynamic range, and overall image quality that Nikon cameras produced. However, seven years ago, I made the switch to Sony due to their innovative technology and compact mirrorless cameras. While I enjoyed using Sony gear, I started to feel like something was missing in my images.

After much contemplation, I decided to give Nikon another chance. I purchased the Nikon D850, one of their flagship DSLR cameras, and I have been blown away by the results. The color rendition, dynamic range, and overall image quality have surpassed my expectations. In this blog post, I will share some practical tips for photographers who are considering making the switch to Nikon after using Sony.

## Practical Tips

### 1. Familiarize Yourself with the Menu System

One of the first things you will notice when switching from Sony to Nikon is the difference in menu systems. Nikon's menu system can be a bit overwhelming at first, but with some practice, you will quickly become familiar with it. Take the time to explore all the settings and customization options available on your Nikon camera to make the most of its capabilities.

### 2. Invest in Quality Lenses

One of the key advantages of Nikon cameras is their wide range of high-quality lenses. Invest in good lenses that suit your style of photography to achieve the best results. Whether you are into landscape photography, portraits, or wildlife, Nikon has a lens for every need. Consider purchasing prime lenses for superior image quality and faster aperture settings.

### 3. Experiment with Different Settings

Nikon cameras offer a wide range of settings and customization options to cater to different shooting scenarios. Experiment with different settings such as aperture, shutter speed, and ISO to understand how they affect your images. Take the time to practice shooting in different lighting conditions to familiarize yourself with your Nikon camera's capabilities.

### 4. Take Advantage of Nikon's Image Quality

One of the main reasons I decided to switch back to Nikon was for their superior image quality. Nikon cameras produce stunning images with excellent color rendition and dynamic range. Take advantage of Nikon's image quality by shooting in RAW format and post-processing your images to bring out the best in them.

### 5. Join Nikon Communities and Workshops

To further enhance your skills as a Nikon photographer, consider joining Nikon communities and workshops. These platforms provide a great opportunity to connect with other Nikon users, share tips and techniques, and learn from experienced photographers. Networking with other Nikon users can also open up new opportunities for collaborations and projects.

## Conclusion

Switching from Sony to Nikon after seven years has been a rewarding experience for me as a photographer. I have rediscovered my love for Nikon's image quality and have enjoyed experimenting with their wide range of lenses and settings. If you are considering making the switch to Nikon, I highly recommend taking the plunge and exploring all that Nikon cameras have to offer.

In conclusion, choosing the right camera gear is a personal decision that should be based on your individual preferences and shooting style. Whether you are a loyal Sony user or considering switching to Nikon, the most important thing is to choose gear that enables you to express your creativity and achieve your photographic vision. Happy shooting!
