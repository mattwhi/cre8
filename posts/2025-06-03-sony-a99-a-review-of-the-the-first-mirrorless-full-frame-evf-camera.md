# Sony A99 – a review of the first mirrorless full-frame EVF camera

In the world of photography, Sony has always been a major player, known for its innovative technology and cutting-edge cameras. The Sony A99 is no exception. This mirrorless full-frame EVF camera has taken the photography world by storm, offering photographers a new level of control and creativity.

## Introduction

The Sony A99 is a game-changer in the world of photography. It combines the best features of a mirrorless camera with a full-frame sensor, giving photographers the best of both worlds. With its electronic viewfinder (EVF) and advanced autofocus system, the A99 offers unparalleled image quality and control. 

![Sony A99](/path/to/sony-a99.jpg)

## Practical Tips

### 1. Take Advantage of the EVF
The electronic viewfinder on the Sony A99 allows you to see exactly what your final image will look like before you even press the shutter button. Take advantage of this feature by adjusting your settings in real-time and ensuring you get the perfect shot every time.

### 2. Experiment with Autofocus
The A99’s advanced autofocus system is incredibly fast and accurate. Experiment with different focus modes and tracking settings to find the one that works best for your shooting style. Whether you’re shooting fast-moving subjects or capturing intricate details, the autofocus system on the A99 will not disappoint.

### 3. Use the Full-Frame Sensor to Your Advantage
The full-frame sensor on the A99 allows for stunning image quality and detail. Take advantage of this by experimenting with different lighting conditions and compositions. The A99’s sensor can handle low light situations with ease, making it perfect for shooting in challenging environments.

### 4. Customize Your Controls
The A99 offers a high degree of customization, allowing you to tailor the camera to your specific shooting needs. Take the time to set up your preferred shooting modes, custom buttons, and menu settings to streamline your workflow and make shooting with the A99 a breeze.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, the Sony A99 is a groundbreaking camera that offers photographers a new level of control and creativity. With its mirrorless design, full-frame sensor, and advanced autofocus system, the A99 is a versatile tool that can handle any shooting situation with ease. Whether you’re a professional photographer looking for a new challenge or an amateur looking to take your photography to the next level, the Sony A99 is a camera worth considering.
