# Binstax: Using a Fuji Instax Square adapter for Mamiya RB67

In the world of photography, there is a constant desire to bridge the gap between the old and the new. One such innovation is the Binstax, a Fuji Instax Square adapter that allows photographers to use their beloved Mamiya RB67 medium format camera with the popular Instax Square film. This opens up a whole new world of creative possibilities for photographers who want to combine the unmatched image quality of the RB67 with the instant gratification of Instax Square prints.

## Introduction

The Mamiya RB67 is a legendary medium format camera known for its exceptional image quality and versatility. However, one of the drawbacks of using the RB67 is the lack of instant feedback that modern digital cameras offer. This is where the Binstax adapter comes in. By attaching the Binstax adapter to your RB67, you can instantly capture and print your images on Instax Square film, giving you the best of both worlds.

## Practical Tips

Here are some practical tips for using the Binstax adapter with your Mamiya RB67:

1. **Proper Installation:** Make sure to carefully follow the instructions for attaching the Binstax adapter to your RB67. Improper installation can lead to light leaks and other issues that can affect the quality of your images.

2. **Film Loading:** When loading Instax Square film into the adapter, make sure to do so in a dark or dimly lit environment to avoid exposing the film to light. Follow the instructions provided with the adapter to ensure proper loading.

3. **Focusing:** The RB67 is a manual focus camera, so take your time to ensure that your subject is sharp and in focus before taking the shot. Instax Square film is not forgiving when it comes to focus errors, so precision is key.

4. **Exposure Settings:** Experiment with different exposure settings to find the right balance for your images. Instax Square film has a limited dynamic range, so be mindful of highlights and shadows in your compositions.

5. **Patience:** Instant photography is all about embracing the imperfections and surprises that come with the medium. Embrace the unpredictability of Instax Square film and let it inspire your creativity.
