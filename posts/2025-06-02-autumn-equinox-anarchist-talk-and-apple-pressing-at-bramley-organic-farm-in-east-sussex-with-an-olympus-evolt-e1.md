# Autumn Equinox Anarchist Talk and Apple Pressing at Bramley Organic Farm in East Sussex with an Olympus Evolt E1

As autumn approaches, the crisp air and changing leaves signal the time for harvest festivals and seasonal gatherings. One such event that combines nature, community, and photography is the Autumn Equinox Anarchist Talk and Apple Pressing at Bramley Organic Farm in East Sussex. This unique event brings together activists, artists, and photographers to celebrate the changing of the seasons and the abundance of the harvest.

## Introduction to the Event

The Autumn Equinox Anarchist Talk and Apple Pressing at Bramley Organic Farm is a gathering that celebrates the principles of sustainability, community, and self-sufficiency. The event begins with a series of talks and workshops led by local activists and artists, covering topics such as permaculture, food sovereignty, and environmental justice. Participants are encouraged to engage in discussions, ask questions, and share their own experiences and knowledge.

After the talks, attendees have the opportunity to participate in apple pressing, a traditional method of extracting juice from freshly harvested apples. This hands-on activity allows participants to learn about the process of making apple cider and to enjoy the fruits of their labor. The event also includes live music, art installations, and a communal meal made from locally sourced ingredients.

### Practical Tips for Photographing the Event

1. **Capture the Details**: Take close-up shots of the apple pressing process, focusing on the hands of participants as they work with the apples and the machinery. Look for interesting textures and patterns in the apples and the equipment used for pressing.

2. **Embrace Natural Light**: The soft, golden light of autumn can create beautiful and atmospheric images. Take advantage of the natural light during the event, positioning yourself to capture the warm glow of the setting sun or the dappled light filtering through the trees.

3. **Document the Community**: Photograph the interactions between participants, capturing the sense of camaraderie and shared purpose that defines the event. Look for moments of connection, collaboration, and celebration that showcase the spirit of community.

4. **Experiment with Composition**: Play with different angles, perspectives, and framing techniques to create dynamic and visually interesting images. Try shooting from low angles to capture the action from a unique perspective or use leading lines to draw the viewer's eye through the frame.

5. **Tell a Story**: Use your images to tell a narrative of the event, capturing the progression from the talks and workshops to the apple pressing and communal meal. Look for moments of emotion, connection, and transformation that convey the essence of the event.
