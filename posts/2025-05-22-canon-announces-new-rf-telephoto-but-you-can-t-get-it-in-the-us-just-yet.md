# Canon Announces New RF Telephoto, But You Can’t Get It in the US Just Yet

Canon has recently unveiled its latest addition to the RF lens lineup, a new telephoto lens that promises to deliver stunning images with exceptional clarity and detail. The RF 800mm f/11 IS STM and RF 600mm f/11 IS STM are both lightweight and compact super-telephoto lenses that are perfect for wildlife and sports photography. However, there's a catch - these lenses are currently only available in select markets and not yet in the US.

## Introduction to the New RF Telephoto Lenses

The new RF 800mm f/11 IS STM and RF 600mm f/11 IS STM lenses are designed to provide photographers with long reach and high image quality while remaining portable and easy to use. The fixed f/11 aperture helps to keep the lenses compact and lightweight, making them ideal for shooting on the go. Both lenses feature built-in image stabilization to help reduce camera shake and ensure sharp images even at long focal lengths.

## Key Features of the RF Telephoto Lenses

- Lightweight and compact design
- Fixed f/11 aperture for portability
- Image stabilization for sharp images
- Smooth and quiet STM focusing motor
- Compatible with Canon RF mount cameras

## Practical Tips for Using Telephoto Lenses

When shooting with a telephoto lens, there are a few tips and tricks that can help you get the best results:

1. Use a sturdy tripod to stabilize the camera and lens, especially when shooting at long focal lengths.
2. Experiment with different apertures to find the sweet spot for sharpness and depth of field.
3. Keep an eye on your shutter speed to prevent camera shake and motion blur.
4. Use manual focus for precise control over your shots, especially when shooting moving subjects.
5. Practice your technique to improve your skills and get the most out of your telephoto lens.

## Conclusion

While the new Canon RF telephoto lenses may not be available in the US just yet, they are definitely worth keeping an eye out for. In the meantime, photographers can start preparing for the arrival of these lenses by honing their skills and getting familiar with telephoto shooting techniques.
