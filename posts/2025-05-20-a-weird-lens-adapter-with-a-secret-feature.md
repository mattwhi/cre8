Shifting the lens allows you to correct perspective distortion or create interesting compositions.

This feature opens up a whole new world of creative possibilities for photographers. Whether you're shooting landscapes, portraits, or architecture, the tilt-shift function can help you achieve stunning and unique results that would be difficult to replicate with a regular lens.
