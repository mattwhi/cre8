# 5 85mm Lenses Perfect For Portrait Photography - The Phoblographer

Portrait photography is a popular genre among photographers, as it allows them to capture the essence and personality of their subjects in a single frame. One of the key components of a successful portrait is the lens used to capture the image. An 85mm lens is a popular choice among portrait photographers, as it offers a flattering focal length that is ideal for capturing sharp, detailed portraits with beautiful bokeh.

In this blog post, we will discuss five 85mm lenses that are perfect for portrait photography, as recommended by The Phoblographer. From budget-friendly options to high-end professional lenses, there is something for every photographer looking to up their portrait game. 

## Introduction

Portrait photography is all about capturing the essence of a person. Whether you are shooting a headshot, a family portrait, or a fashion editorial, the right lens can make all the difference in the final result. An 85mm lens is a versatile option that is widely used by portrait photographers for its flattering focal length and ability to produce stunning bokeh. 

## Practical Tips

When shooting portraits with an 85mm lens, there are a few practical tips to keep in mind to ensure you get the best results. Here are some tips to help you make the most of your 85mm lens:

1. **Use a wide aperture**: One of the key advantages of an 85mm lens is its ability to produce beautiful bokeh. To make the most of this, shoot at a wide aperture (f/1.8 or wider) to create a shallow depth of field and make your subject stand out from the background.

2. **Focus on the eyes**: When shooting portraits, it is essential to focus on the subject's eyes to create a connection with the viewer. Use the autofocus capabilities of your lens to ensure the eyes are sharp and in focus.

3. **Consider the distance**: The 85mm focal length is ideal for shooting portraits at a comfortable distance from your subject. It allows you to capture intimate shots without being too close or too far away.

4. **Experiment with angles**: Don't be afraid to experiment with different angles and perspectives when shooting portraits with an 85mm lens. Try shooting from above, below, or from the side to create unique and dynamic compositions.

## 5 85mm Lenses Perfect For Portrait Photography

1. **Canon EF 85mm f/1.8 USM**: This budget-friendly lens from Canon is a popular choice among portrait photographers for its sharpness, fast autofocus, and beautiful bokeh. It is a versatile option that is suitable for both full-frame and crop sensor cameras.

2. **Nikon AF-S NIKKOR 85mm f/1.8G**: Nikon's 85mm f/1.8G lens is another excellent choice for portrait photography, offering sharpness, fast autofocus, and stunning image quality. It is compatible with both full-frame and crop sensor Nikon cameras.

3. **Sony FE 85mm f/1.8**: Sony's 85mm f/1.8 lens is a great option for Sony E-mount users looking for a lightweight and compact portrait lens. It offers excellent sharpness, fast autofocus, and beautiful bokeh, making it ideal for portrait photography.

4. **Sigma 85mm f/1.4 DG HSM Art**: Sigma's 85mm f/1.4 Art lens is a high-end option for portrait photographers looking for professional-quality images. It offers exceptional sharpness, beautiful bokeh, and fast autofocus, making it a favorite among portrait photographers.

5. **Tamron SP 85mm f/1.8 Di VC USD**: Tamron's 85mm f/1.8 lens is a versatile option that offers image stabilization, fast autofocus, and excellent image quality. It is a great choice for portrait photographers looking for a reliable and high-quality lens.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, an 85mm lens is a versatile and popular choice for portrait photography, offering a flattering focal length and beautiful bokeh. Whether you are a beginner looking for a budget-friendly option or a professional photographer in need of a high-end lens, there is a perfect 85mm lens for you. Consider the five lenses mentioned in this blog post as recommendations to help you take your portrait photography to the next level. Happy shooting!
