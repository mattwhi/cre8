## Practical Tips

Here are some practical tips for getting the most out of your Pentax M 28mm 2.8 lens:

1. **Use a tripod:** To ensure sharp images, especially in low light conditions, use a tripod to stabilize your camera.
   
2. **Experiment with different angles:** Don't be afraid to get low to the ground or shoot from higher angles to create unique perspectives.
   
3. **Pay attention to the corners:** The Pentax M 28mm 2.8 lens is known for its sharpness, but make sure to check the corners of your frame for any distortion or vignetting.
   
4. **Shoot in RAW:** Shooting in RAW format will give you more flexibility in post-processing and allow you to make adjustments to the exposure and colors.
