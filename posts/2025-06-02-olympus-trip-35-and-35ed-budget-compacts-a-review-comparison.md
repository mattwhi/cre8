# Olympus Trip 35 and 35ED – Budget Compacts – a Review & Comparison

In the world of film photography, compact cameras are highly sought after for their portability and ease of use. Two popular options for budget-conscious photographers are the Olympus Trip 35 and the Olympus 35ED. Both cameras offer excellent image quality and features that make them perfect for everyday shooting. In this review, we will compare the two cameras and help you decide which one is right for you.

## Introduction

The Olympus Trip 35 and 35ED are both compact 35mm film cameras that were popular in the 1980s. The Trip 35 is a classic point-and-shoot camera that is known for its simplicity and reliability. The 35ED, on the other hand, offers a bit more control over exposure settings and is favored by photographers who prefer manual adjustments.

### Olympus Trip 35

The Olympus Trip 35 is a simple yet effective camera that is perfect for beginners or photographers who want a hassle-free shooting experience. The camera features a fixed 40mm f/2.8 lens, which produces sharp and vibrant images. The Trip 35 also has a fully automatic exposure system, which makes it easy to use in a variety of lighting conditions.

### Olympus 35ED

The Olympus 35ED is a slightly more advanced version of the Trip 35, featuring a 35mm f/2.8 lens and manual exposure settings. This camera allows photographers to have more control over their images, making it a great choice for those who want to experiment with different shooting techniques. The 35ED also has a built-in flash, which is perfect for low-light situations.

## Practical Tips

When using either the Olympus Trip 35 or 35ED, there are a few tips that can help you get the most out of your shooting experience:

1. **Use a high-quality film**: Both cameras produce excellent results with high-quality film. Experiment with different brands and speeds to find the one that works best for your style of photography.

2. **Experiment with different settings**: While the Trip 35 is fully automatic, the 35ED allows for manual adjustments. Take the time to experiment with different settings to see how they affect your images.

3. **Take care of your camera**: These cameras are durable, but they still require proper care and maintenance. Keep your camera clean and store it in a safe place when not in use.

## Comparison

When comparing the Olympus Trip 35 and 35ED, there are a few key differences to consider:

1. **Lens**: The Trip 35 has a fixed 40mm lens, while the 35ED has a 35mm lens. Both lenses produce sharp images, but the 35ED offers a slightly wider field of view.

2. **Exposure Settings**: The Trip 35 is fully automatic, while the 35ED allows for manual adjustments. If you prefer more control over your images, the 35ED may be the better option for you.

3. **Flash**: The 35ED has a built-in flash, which can be useful in low-light situations. The Trip 35 does not have a built-in flash, but external flashes can be used with both cameras.

## Conclusion

Both the Olympus Trip 35 and 35ED are excellent budget compact cameras that offer great image quality and ease of use. The Trip 35 is perfect for beginners or photographers who prefer a simple shooting experience, while the 35ED is ideal for those who want more control over their images. Whichever camera you choose, you can't go wrong with either of these classic Olympus models. Happy shooting!
