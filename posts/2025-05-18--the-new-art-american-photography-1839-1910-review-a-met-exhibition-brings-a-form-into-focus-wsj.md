# ‘The New Art: American Photography, 1839-1910’ Review: A Met Exhibition Brings a Form Into Focus - WSJ

Are you a photography enthusiast looking to gain inspiration from the early days of American photography? If so, you will not want to miss the latest exhibition at The Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City. Titled ‘The New Art: American Photography, 1839-1910,’ this exhibition showcases a wide range of photographs from the early days of the medium, highlighting the evolution of American photography during this period.

In this blog post, we will provide a comprehensive review of the exhibition, including key takeaways for photographers looking to learn from the pioneers of the art form. From practical tips to insights on composition and technique, we will delve into the world of early American photography and explore how it continues to influence the medium today.

## The Exhibition: A Closer Look

![The New Art Exhibition](/path/to/image)

The ‘The New Art: American Photography, 1839-1910’ exhibition at The Met is a comprehensive showcase of the early days of American photography. The exhibition features works from pioneering photographers such as Mathew Brady, Alfred Stieglitz, and Edward Steichen, among others. From daguerreotypes to early gelatin silver prints, the exhibition offers a glimpse into the diverse range of techniques and styles that emerged during this period.

One of the standout features of the exhibition is its focus on the role of American photographers in shaping the medium. From documenting the Civil War to capturing everyday life in urban centers, the photographs on display offer a unique perspective on American history and culture during the 19th and early 20th centuries.

## Practical Tips for Photographers

For photographers looking to gain inspiration from the early days of American photography, there are several practical tips that can be gleaned from the exhibition. Here are a few key takeaways:

1. **Composition is Key:** Many of the photographs on display at the exhibition showcase the importance of composition in creating compelling images. Pay attention to the way the photographers frame their subjects and use leading lines to draw the viewer's eye to the focal point of the image.

2. **Experiment with Different Techniques:** The early pioneers of American photography were not afraid to experiment with new techniques and processes. Take inspiration from their willingness to push the boundaries of the medium and try out new techniques in your own photography practice.

3. **Capture Everyday Moments:** Some of the most powerful images in the exhibition are those that capture everyday moments in American life. Look for opportunities to document the world around you in a way that is authentic and true to the spirit of the moment.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, ‘The New Art: American Photography, 1839-1910’ exhibition at The Met is a must-see for photography enthusiasts looking to gain inspiration from the early days of the medium. From the diverse range of techniques and styles on display to the powerful storytelling inherent in the images, there is much to learn from the pioneers of American photography.

By taking the time to visit the exhibition and study the works on display, photographers can gain valuable insights into the evolution of the medium and how it continues to shape the way we see the world today. So, if you find yourself in New York City, be sure to make a stop at The Met and experience the magic of early American photography for yourself.
