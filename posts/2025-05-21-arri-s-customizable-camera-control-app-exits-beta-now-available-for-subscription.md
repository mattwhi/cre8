# ARRI’s Customizable Camera Control App Exits BETA, Now Available for Subscription

Photographers and filmmakers rejoice! ARRI, the renowned camera manufacturer, has officially launched their customizable camera control app out of BETA and is now available for subscription. This innovative app allows users to remotely control their ARRI cameras from their mobile devices, providing a convenient and efficient way to manage settings and capture stunning images and videos. In this blog post, we will explore the features of ARRI’s customizable camera control app, provide practical tips for utilizing its capabilities, and discuss the benefits of incorporating this tool into your photography workflow.

## Introduction to ARRI's Customizable Camera Control App

ARRI's customizable camera control app is designed to streamline the process of capturing high-quality images and videos with ARRI cameras. The app allows users to remotely adjust camera settings such as focus, exposure, white balance, and more, directly from their mobile devices. This level of control enables photographers and filmmakers to fine-tune their shots without having to physically interact with the camera, saving time and ensuring greater precision in their work.

With the app's user-friendly interface, users can easily navigate through different settings and make adjustments on the fly. Additionally, the app offers customizable control layouts, allowing users to create their own personalized control panels tailored to their specific shooting needs. This level of customization empowers users to optimize their workflow and enhance their creative vision.

To make the most of ARRI's customizable camera control app, consider the following practical tips:

2. **Experiment with Customization**: Play around with the app's customizable control layouts to create a setup that works best for your shooting style. Consider grouping related settings together for easier access during a shoot.

3. **Practice Remote Control**: Test out the app's remote control capabilities by adjusting camera settings from a distance. This can be particularly useful for capturing shots in challenging or hard-to-reach locations.

4. **Stay Updated**: Keep an eye out for app updates and new features that ARRI may release in the future. Stay informed about any improvements or enhancements that could further enhance your shooting experience.

## Benefits of Incorporating ARRI's Camera Control App into Your Workflow

Integrating ARRI's customizable camera control app into your photography workflow offers a range of benefits, including:

1. **Enhanced Efficiency**: By being able to remotely control your camera settings, you can make quick adjustments without interrupting your shooting flow. This can save time and ensure that you capture the perfect shot.

4. **Convenience**: The convenience of being able to control your ARRI camera from your mobile device adds flexibility to your shooting process. Whether you're shooting on location or in a studio, the app provides a convenient way to manage your camera settings.

In conclusion, ARRI's customizable camera control app offers a versatile and user-friendly solution for photographers and filmmakers looking to enhance their shooting experience. With its remote control capabilities, customizable control layouts, and range of features, the app provides a powerful tool for optimizing your workflow and unlocking new creative possibilities.
