While many people claim to be able to see or sense auras, the existence of these energy fields has long been dismissed by the scientific community as mere pseudoscience.

## The Phantom Leaf Effect

In the 1970s, a scientist named Thelma Moss conducted a series of experiments that would come to be known as the "phantom leaf effect." Moss took high-speed photographs of leaves before and after they were cut from a plant. Surprisingly, the photographs of the cut leaves showed a faint outline of the missing leaf, as if the energy field of the leaf was still present.
