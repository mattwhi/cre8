# Michael Jackson Biopic Moonwalking to 22 Days of Additional Photography in June (Exclusive) - The Hollywood Reporter

The highly anticipated Michael Jackson biopic is currently in production, and The Hollywood Reporter has exclusively revealed that the film will undergo 22 days of additional photography in the month of June. This news has sparked excitement among fans and industry professionals alike, as the biopic promises to offer a unique and intimate look into the life of the King of Pop.

As a photographer, this presents a fantastic opportunity to capture behind-the-scenes moments of the production and potentially contribute to the visual storytelling of the film. In this blog post, we will explore some practical tips for photographers looking to make the most of this exciting opportunity.

## Practical Tips for Photographers

1. **Get Permission**: Before attempting to capture any behind-the-scenes moments of the Michael Jackson biopic, make sure to obtain the necessary permissions from the production team. This will ensure that you are not infringing on any copyright laws and will allow you to work freely within the production environment.

2. **Capture Candid Moments**: One of the most exciting aspects of behind-the-scenes photography is the ability to capture candid moments that may not make it into the final film. Look for opportunities to photograph the cast and crew interacting naturally, as these moments can provide a unique insight into the production process.

3. **Experiment with Different Angles**: Don't be afraid to experiment with different angles and perspectives when photographing behind the scenes. Try shooting from low angles or using a wide-angle lens to create dynamic and visually interesting images.

4. **Focus on Details**: Pay attention to the small details that help to tell the story of the production. This could be a close-up of a costume or props, or a shot of the set design. These details can help to create a more immersive experience for viewers of your photographs.

5. **Stay Flexible**: Production schedules can be unpredictable, so be prepared to adapt to changes and new opportunities as they arise. Stay flexible and open to new ideas, as this can lead to some of the most memorable and impactful images.
