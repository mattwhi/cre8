# Maya Shkolnik, CC ’25, on Photography, Psychoanalysis, and Protest Art - Columbia Daily Spectator

---

Photography has always been a powerful tool for self-expression and social commentary. For Maya Shkolnik, a freshman at Columbia College, it is a medium through which she explores the intersection of art, psychoanalysis, and protest. In a recent interview with the Columbia Daily Spectator, Maya shared her thoughts on how photography can be used as a tool for activism and personal growth.

![Maya Shkolnik](/images/maya_shkolnik.jpg)

---

## The Intersection of Photography and Psychoanalysis

Maya's interest in psychoanalysis began long before she picked up a camera. She believes that our subconscious thoughts and emotions are often reflected in the images we create. By exploring the deeper layers of the mind through photography, Maya hopes to bring awareness to issues that are often overlooked in mainstream media.

According to Maya, "Photography allows us to capture fleeting moments and emotions that are often hidden beneath the surface. By delving into the subconscious mind, we can uncover truths about ourselves and the world around us."

---

## Using Photography for Protest Art

One of Maya's most powerful projects is her series on protest art. Through her lens, she captures the raw emotions and energy of demonstrations, highlighting the voices of those who are often marginalized in society. By shining a spotlight on these movements, Maya hopes to inspire others to take action and stand up for what they believe in.

"Protest art has the power to spark change and challenge the status quo," Maya explains. "Through my photography, I aim to amplify the voices of those who are fighting for justice and equality."

---

## Practical Tips for Aspiring Photographers

For aspiring photographers who are interested in using their art for activism, Maya offers some practical tips:

1. **Find Your Passion:** Choose subjects that resonate with you on a personal level. Your passion will shine through in your work.
2. **Tell a Story:** Every photograph has a story to tell. Think about the message you want to convey before you press the shutter button.
3. **Experiment:** Don't be afraid to try new techniques and styles. Photography is a journey of self-discovery.
4. **Stay Informed:** Keep up to date with current events and social issues. Your photography can be a powerful tool for raising awareness.

---

## In Conclusion

Maya Shkolnik's work is a testament to the power of photography as a form of protest and self-expression. Through her art, she challenges us to think critically about the world we live in and encourages us to take action. As photographers, we have a unique opportunity to make a difference and create change through our images.

So the next time you pick up your camera, think about the stories you want to tell and the impact you want to make. Like Maya, let your photography be a reflection of your beliefs and a catalyst for positive change in the world.
