# FilmConvert and CineMatch Roll Out Support for Blackmagic PYXIS 6K

Exciting news for photographers and filmmakers using the Blackmagic PYXIS 6K camera - FilmConvert and CineMatch have just announced support for this cutting-edge device. This means you can now easily achieve stunning color grading and matching results with your PYXIS 6K footage, taking your projects to the next level. In this blog post, we will delve into the details of this new support and provide practical tips for maximizing your results.

## Introduction to FilmConvert and CineMatch

FilmConvert and CineMatch are renowned tools in the world of color grading and matching. FilmConvert offers a range of film stock emulation profiles that can be applied to your footage to achieve a cinematic look. On the other hand, CineMatch specializes in accurately matching the color science of different cameras, ensuring seamless integration of footage from multiple sources.

With the addition of support for the Blackmagic PYXIS 6K camera, users can now harness the power of these tools to elevate their projects to new heights. Whether you are shooting a feature film, a commercial, or a music video, having access to FilmConvert and CineMatch support for the PYXIS 6K opens up a world of creative possibilities.

## Practical Tips for Using FilmConvert and CineMatch with the Blackmagic PYXIS 6K

To make the most of FilmConvert and CineMatch support for the Blackmagic PYXIS 6K, here are some practical tips to keep in mind:

1. **Shoot in a Flat Profile**: To give yourself more flexibility in post-production, consider shooting in a flat profile on your PYXIS 6K camera. This will allow you to have more dynamic range to work with when applying color grading and matching effects.

2. **Use FilmConvert Profiles**: Experiment with different film stock emulation profiles in FilmConvert to find the look that best suits your project. Whether you prefer the warmth of Kodak or the cool tones of Fuji, FilmConvert has a wide range of options to choose from.

3. **Match Footage with CineMatch**: If you are using footage from multiple cameras in your project, use CineMatch to ensure a seamless color match. Simply select the cameras you are working with, and CineMatch will do the rest, ensuring a consistent look across all your footage.

4. **Fine-Tune Your Grades**: Don't be afraid to experiment with the various controls and settings in FilmConvert and CineMatch. Adjusting parameters such as exposure, contrast, saturation, and temperature can help you achieve the perfect look for your project.
