# Panasonic Announces Next Gen S1 in Two Models, Plus a New Lens

Panasonic has just announced the release of their next generation S1 camera in two models, along with a new lens that is sure to excite photographers everywhere. The S1 has been highly anticipated by the photography community, and Panasonic has not disappointed with their latest offerings.

![Panasonic S1](panasonic_s1.jpg)

## Introduction
The Panasonic S1 has been a favorite among photographers for its high-quality images, robust build, and advanced features. With the release of the next generation S1, Panasonic is taking things to the next level with two new models that cater to different types of photographers. In addition, they have also introduced a new lens that promises to enhance the capabilities of the S1 even further.

## New Models
The two new models of the Panasonic S1 are the S1R and the S1H. The S1R is geared towards high-resolution photography with its 47.3-megapixel sensor, while the S1H is designed for videographers with its 6K video recording capabilities. Both models feature improved autofocus, better low-light performance, and enhanced image stabilization.

## Practical Tips
If you are considering purchasing one of the new Panasonic S1 models, here are a few practical tips to keep in mind:

1. **Invest in Quality Lenses**: The new S1 models are capable of capturing incredibly detailed images, so it's worth investing in high-quality lenses to make the most of their capabilities.
2. **Experiment with Different Shooting Modes**: Take the time to explore the various shooting modes and settings on the S1 to discover what works best for your style of photography.
3. **Utilize the Advanced Autofocus Features**: The improved autofocus on the S1 models allows for fast and accurate focusing, so be sure to take advantage of this feature in your photography.

## New Lens
In addition to the new S1 models, Panasonic has also announced a new lens - the Lumix S 24-105mm f/4 Macro O.I.S. This versatile lens is perfect for a wide range of shooting scenarios, from landscape photography to portraits. The macro capabilities of the lens make it ideal for capturing close-up details with stunning clarity.

## Conclusion
The release of the next generation Panasonic S1 models and the new Lumix lens is sure to excite photographers looking to elevate their craft. With improved performance, advanced features, and exceptional image quality, the S1 continues to solidify its position as a top choice for professionals and enthusiasts alike. Whether you are a still photographer or a videographer, there is a Panasonic S1 model that will meet your needs and exceed your expectations.
