# Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 Review | A Classic 35mm Prime For Crop Sensors

Are you a photographer looking for a versatile prime lens for your crop sensor camera? Look no further than the Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7. This lens offers a classic 35mm focal length equivalent with a fast aperture, making it a perfect choice for a wide range of photography styles. In this review, we will dive deep into the features, performance, and practical tips for using this lens.

## Introduction to the Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7

The Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 is a compact and lightweight prime lens designed specifically for crop sensor cameras. With a 35mm equivalent focal length, this lens is ideal for street photography, landscapes, portraits, and everyday shooting. The fast f/1.7 aperture allows for great low-light performance and beautiful bokeh, making it a versatile option for a wide variety of situations.

![Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7](/path/to/image)

### Key Features:
- Fast f/1.7 aperture
- Compact and lightweight design
- 35mm equivalent focal length
- Silent autofocus motor
- Weather-sealed construction

## Performance

The Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 delivers outstanding performance in terms of image quality and autofocus speed. The fast aperture allows for excellent low-light performance, while the silent autofocus motor ensures quick and accurate focusing. The lens produces sharp images with beautiful bokeh, making it a great option for portrait and street photography.

### Image Quality
The lens produces sharp images with minimal distortion and chromatic aberration. The colors are vibrant and true to life, making post-processing a breeze. The fast aperture allows for great subject isolation and creamy bokeh, perfect for creating stunning portraits.

### Autofocus
The autofocus performance of the Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 is fast and accurate, thanks to the silent autofocus motor. Whether you are shooting portraits or capturing fast-moving subjects, this lens will ensure you never miss a moment.

## Practical Tips for Using the Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7

Here are some practical tips for getting the most out of your Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 lens:

1. **Experiment with Different Apertures**: The fast f/1.7 aperture allows for creative control over depth of field. Try shooting wide open for beautiful bokeh or stop down for greater depth of field.

2. **Use It for Street Photography**: The 35mm equivalent focal length is perfect for street photography. The compact size of the lens makes it discreet and easy to carry around for capturing candid moments.

3. **Shoot in Low Light**: The fast aperture of the lens makes it ideal for shooting in low light conditions. Use a higher ISO setting to capture sharp images without sacrificing image quality.

4. **Pair It with a Crop Sensor Camera**: The Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 is designed specifically for crop sensor cameras. Pairing it with a compatible camera will ensure optimal performance and image quality.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, the Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 is a versatile and high-performing prime lens for crop sensor cameras. With its fast aperture, compact design, and outstanding image quality, this lens is a great choice for photographers looking for a classic 35mm prime lens. Whether you are shooting portraits, landscapes, or street photography, the Nikon Z DX 24mm f/1.7 will not disappoint. So, grab yourself a copy of this lens and start capturing stunning images today!
