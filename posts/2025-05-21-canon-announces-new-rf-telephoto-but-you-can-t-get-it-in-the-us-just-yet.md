# Canon Announces New RF Telephoto, But You Can’t Get It in the US Just Yet

Great news for photographers around the world, Canon has announced a new RF telephoto lens that is sure to take your photography to the next level. The RF 800mm F5.6L IS USM and RF 1200mm F8L IS USM are the latest additions to Canon's RF lens lineup, offering incredible reach and image quality for capturing distant subjects.

## Introducing the RF 800mm F5.6L IS USM and RF 1200mm F8L IS USM

The RF 800mm F5.6L IS USM and RF 1200mm F8L IS USM are both designed for professional photographers who need the ultimate telephoto reach. With a focal length of 800mm and 1200mm respectively, these lenses are perfect for wildlife, sports, and astrophotography.

Both lenses feature Canon's renowned L-series optics, providing exceptional sharpness and clarity. The built-in Image Stabilization (IS) system ensures steady shots even when shooting handheld or in low light conditions. Additionally, the lenses are weather-sealed for durability in challenging environments.

## Practical Tips for Using Telephoto Lenses

When shooting with a telephoto lens, there are a few tips and techniques that can help you get the best results:

1. **Use a tripod**: When using a telephoto lens with a long focal length, even the slightest movement can result in blurry images. Using a sturdy tripod will help stabilize your camera and ensure sharp photos.

2. **Focus carefully**: With a narrow depth of field, it's important to focus carefully on your subject. Use autofocus or manual focus to ensure your subject is sharp.

3. **Watch your shutter speed**: To avoid camera shake and motion blur, make sure to use a fast shutter speed when shooting with a telephoto lens. A good rule of thumb is to use a shutter speed that is at least as fast as the focal length of your lens.

4. **Consider the background**: With a telephoto lens, you can easily blur the background to make your subject stand out. Pay attention to the background and try to find a clean, uncluttered backdrop for your photos.

## Conclusion

While the new RF telephoto lenses from Canon offer incredible reach and image quality, they are currently only available in select markets outside of the US. However, with their announcement, it's only a matter of time before these lenses make their way to photographers in the US.
