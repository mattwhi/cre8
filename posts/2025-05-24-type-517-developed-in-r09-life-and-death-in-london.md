# Type 517 developed in R09 – Life and Death in London

London is a city of contrasts. From the bustling streets of Soho to the quiet alleys of Hampstead, there is always something new to discover. As a photographer, capturing the essence of London can be both challenging and rewarding. One way to add a unique touch to your London photos is by using Type 517 film developed in R09. In this blog post, we will explore the characteristics of Type 517 film, share some practical tips for shooting in London, and discuss the life and death themes that can be captured through this film.

## Introduction to Type 517 Film

Type 517 film is a high-contrast black and white film that produces dramatic and gritty images. When developed in R09, also known as Rodinal, the film takes on a unique look with deep blacks and crisp whites. This combination of film and developer can add a sense of nostalgia and rawness to your photos, making them stand out from the crowd.

## Practical Tips for Shooting in London

1. **Explore Different Neighborhoods:** London is a diverse city with a mix of old and new architecture, bustling markets, and serene parks. Take the time to explore different neighborhoods and capture the unique character of each area.

2. **Use Natural Light:** London's weather can be unpredictable, but when the sun does shine, take advantage of the natural light. Experiment with shadows and highlights to create dynamic compositions.

3. **Capture Street Scenes:** London is a city that never sleeps, with people bustling about at all hours of the day. Capture the energy of the streets by photographing the hustle and bustle of daily life.

4. **Look for Contrast:** London is a city of contrasts, from the modern skyscrapers of the City to the historic buildings of Westminster. Look for juxtapositions of old and new, light and dark, life and death.

5. **Experiment with Type 517 Film:** When shooting in London, consider using Type 517 film developed in R09 for a unique and gritty look. Experiment with different shooting conditions and subjects to see how the film responds.

## Life and Death Themes in London

London is a city steeped in history, with layers of stories waiting to be uncovered. From the ancient Roman ruins of Londinium to the modern skyscrapers of the City, the theme of life and death is ever-present in London. As a photographer, you can capture this duality through your images.

Highgate Cemetery, in particular, is a hauntingly beautiful place filled with elaborate tombs and overgrown vegetation. Use Type 517 film developed in R09 to capture the eerie atmosphere of the cemetery, with its shadows and textures adding to the sense of decay and remembrance.

Another way to explore the theme of life and death in London is by photographing its street art. The vibrant murals and graffiti that adorn the city's walls can be a powerful reminder of the transient nature of life. Use Type 517 film developed in R09 to capture the bold colors and intricate details of the street art, creating a visual juxtaposition of life and death.

London is a city of contrasts, where the themes of life and death are ever-present.
