# Oregon hobbyists reach for the stars with backyard astrophotography - Oregon Public Broadcasting - OPB

In recent years, backyard astrophotography has become a popular hobby among Oregonians looking to capture stunning images of the night sky. With the advancements in technology and accessibility of equipment, amateur photographers are now able to create breathtaking images of stars, planets, and galaxies right from their own backyards. Oregon Public Broadcasting (OPB) recently spoke with some local hobbyists who have taken their love for photography to new heights, quite literally.

## Introduction

Astrophotography is a specialized type of photography that involves capturing images of celestial objects such as stars, planets, and galaxies. It requires specialized equipment such as telescopes, cameras, and tracking mounts to capture these distant objects in stunning detail. While astrophotography was once reserved for professional astronomers, advancements in technology have made it more accessible to amateur photographers.

## Practical Tips for Backyard Astrophotography

### 1. Find a Dark Location

One of the most important factors in astrophotography is finding a location with minimal light pollution. The darker the sky, the clearer the stars will appear in your images. Oregon is known for its stunning natural landscapes and dark skies, making it an ideal location for astrophotography.

### 2. Invest in the Right Equipment

To capture sharp and detailed images of the night sky, you will need the right equipment. A DSLR camera with manual settings, a wide-angle lens, and a sturdy tripod are essential for capturing clear images of stars and planets. Additionally, a telescope and tracking mount can help you capture more detailed images of distant objects.

### 3. Experiment with Different Settings

Astrophotography is a trial-and-error process, so don't be afraid to experiment with different camera settings. Start with a high ISO setting and a wide aperture to capture as much light as possible. You can also try different exposure times to see which settings work best for your specific setup.

### 4. Use Remote Shutter Release

To prevent camera shake and ensure sharp images, use a remote shutter release or the camera's built-in timer to trigger the shutter. This will minimize vibrations and help you capture clear images of the night sky.
