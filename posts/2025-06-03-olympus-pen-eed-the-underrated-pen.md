# Olympus PEN EED – The Underrated PEN

In the world of photography, there are many cameras that have gained fame and recognition over the years. However, there are also some hidden gems that often go unnoticed. One such camera is the **Olympus PEN EED**. This compact film camera may not be as popular as some of its counterparts, but it definitely deserves a second look. In this blog post, we will explore why the Olympus PEN EED is a great choice for photographers looking to experiment with film photography.

## Introduction

The Olympus PEN EED was first released in 1967 and quickly gained a reputation for its compact size and excellent performance. Despite its age, this camera still holds up well in today's digital age. The camera features a fixed 32mm f/1.7 lens, making it perfect for street photography and portraits. The EED also has a built-in light meter, allowing photographers to easily adjust their settings for the perfect exposure.

### Practical Tips

1. **Experiment with Different Films**: One of the great things about shooting with film is the variety of options available. Try experimenting with different types of film to see which ones produce the best results with the Olympus PEN EED.

2. **Take Advantage of the Fixed Lens**: While some photographers may prefer interchangeable lenses, the fixed 32mm lens on the EED can actually be a blessing in disguise. It forces you to get creative with your compositions and can help you develop a unique style.

3. **Master Manual Settings**: The Olympus PEN EED does not have autofocus, so it's important to become comfortable with manual focusing. Practice using the viewfinder to ensure your subjects are in focus before taking the shot.

4. **Utilize the Light Meter**: The built-in light meter on the EED is a handy tool for ensuring proper exposure. Take the time to understand how the light meter works and adjust your settings accordingly.
