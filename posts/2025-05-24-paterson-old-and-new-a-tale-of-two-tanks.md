# Paterson, old and new – A Tale of Two Tanks

Paterson tanks have been a staple in the photography world for decades, providing photographers with a reliable and efficient way to develop their film. In this blog post, we will explore the differences between the old and new Paterson tanks, as well as provide some practical tips for using these essential tools.

## Introduction

For many photographers, developing film is an essential part of the creative process. It allows them to have more control over the final outcome of their images and to experiment with different techniques. Paterson tanks have long been a favorite among photographers for their ease of use and reliable results. 

### Old Paterson Tanks

The old Paterson tanks, such as the Super System 4, have been around for decades and are still popular among film enthusiasts. These tanks are known for their robust construction and ease of use. They typically consist of a tank, a reel, and a funnel for pouring in chemicals. The old Paterson tanks are known for their durability and reliability, making them a favorite among photographers who prefer a more traditional approach to film development.

![Old Paterson Tank](/path/to/old_paterson_tank.jpg)

### New Paterson Tanks

In recent years, Paterson has introduced new models of their tanks, such as the Universal Tank. These tanks have been updated with new features and improvements to make the film developing process even easier and more efficient. The Universal Tank, for example, allows for the development of both 35mm and 120 film without the need for additional reels, making it a versatile option for photographers who shoot with different formats.

![New Paterson Tank](/path/to/new_paterson_tank.jpg)

## Practical Tips for Using Paterson Tanks

1. **Loading the Film:** When loading your film onto the reel, make sure to do it in a dark room or using a changing bag to prevent light leaks. Take your time and be gentle to avoid damaging the film.

2. **Chemical Mixing:** Follow the instructions provided with your Paterson tank for mixing your developer, stop bath, and fixer. Use a timer to ensure that you are agitating the tank at the correct intervals.

3. **Agitation:** Proper agitation is key to ensuring even development of your film. Follow the recommended agitation method for your specific tank to avoid uneven results.

4. **Temperature Control:** Maintain a consistent temperature for your chemicals throughout the development process. Fluctuations in temperature can affect the final outcome of your images.

5. **Rinse Thoroughly:** After fixing your film, make sure to rinse it thoroughly with water to remove any remaining chemicals. This will help prevent stains and ensure the longevity of your negatives.

## Conclusion

Paterson tanks have stood the test of time as reliable tools for film development. Whether you prefer the old-school charm of the Super System 4 or the modern convenience of the Universal Tank, there is a Paterson tank to suit your needs. By following the practical tips outlined in this blog post, you can ensure that your film development process goes smoothly and that you achieve the best possible results with your images. Happy shooting!
