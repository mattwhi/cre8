# Walther Photography Collection Gift to Met Museum to Showcase Over 6,500 Works

The Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City recently announced a generous gift from the Walther Collection. This gift includes over 6,500 works of photography from Africa, Asia, and the Americas, spanning the 19th and 20th centuries. The collection is a significant addition to the Met's already impressive photography holdings and will be showcased in a dedicated gallery space within the museum. This gift represents a major milestone in the recognition of photography as a valuable and important art form.
