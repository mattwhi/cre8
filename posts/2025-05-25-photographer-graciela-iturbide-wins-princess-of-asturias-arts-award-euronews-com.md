## Introduction to Graciela Iturbide

Graciela Iturbide is a Mexican photographer known for her poetic and powerful images that explore the complexities of Mexican culture and identity. Her work often focuses on marginalized communities, particularly indigenous peoples, women, and children. Iturbide's photographs are characterized by their intimate and raw portrayal of her subjects, capturing the beauty and struggles of everyday life.
