# Wooden Camera Kits Out Blackmagic’s Latest Large Format Cine Cameras

In the world of cinematography, having the right equipment can make all the difference when it comes to creating high-quality visuals. Blackmagic Design has been a leader in the industry, known for their innovative and powerful cinema cameras. Their latest large format cine cameras have been making waves in the industry, and now, thanks to Wooden Camera, filmmakers have even more options for customizing and enhancing their setups.

## Introduction

Blackmagic Design recently released their latest large format cine cameras, the URSA Mini Pro 12K and the Pocket Cinema Camera 6K Pro. These cameras offer stunning image quality and impressive features, making them a top choice for many filmmakers. However, like any camera setup, there are always ways to enhance and customize the user experience.

Wooden Camera, a trusted name in the industry for their high-quality camera accessories, has released a range of kits specifically designed for Blackmagic’s latest large format cine cameras. These kits include everything from cages and handles to mounts and power solutions, offering filmmakers the ability to tailor their setups to suit their specific needs.

## Practical Tips

### 1. **Cages and Handles**
One of the key components of Wooden Camera’s kits is the cages and handles. These provide added protection for the camera body while also offering additional mounting points for accessories such as monitors, microphones, and lights. The handles make it easier to carry and maneuver the camera, especially when shooting handheld or on the go.

### 2. **Mounts and Power Solutions**
Wooden Camera’s kits also include mounts and power solutions to help streamline the camera setup process. The mounts allow for easy attachment of accessories such as wireless video transmitters or external recorders, while the power solutions ensure that the camera can run for extended periods without needing to constantly change batteries.

### 3. **Monitor Mounts and EVF Support**
For filmmakers who rely on external monitors or electronic viewfinders (EVFs) for precise framing and focus, Wooden Camera’s kits offer a range of mounting options. These mounts allow for easy attachment of monitors and EVFs to the camera rig, providing a more ergonomic and efficient shooting experience.

### 4. **Lens Support and Follow Focus**
To ensure smooth and accurate focusing, Wooden Camera’s kits also include lens support systems and follow focus units. These accessories help to stabilize the lens and provide precise control over focus adjustments, essential for achieving professional-looking shots.

## Conclusion

Wooden Camera’s kits for Blackmagic’s latest large format cine cameras offer filmmakers a range of customization options to enhance their shooting experience. From cages and handles to mounts and power solutions, these kits provide everything needed to create a professional and efficient camera setup. With the ability to tailor their setups to suit their specific needs, filmmakers can take their cinematography to the next level with Wooden Camera’s accessories.

Whether shooting handheld on location or setting up a complex studio rig, Wooden Camera’s kits provide the versatility and reliability needed to tackle any project with confidence. With their high-quality construction and thoughtful design, these accessories are a must-have for any filmmaker looking to get the most out of their Blackmagic cine camera.
