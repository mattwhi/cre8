## Practical Tips

### Underwater Photography
- **Equipment**: Invest in a good underwater camera or housing to ensure your equipment is protected from water damage.
- **Lighting**: Use natural light whenever possible to enhance the colors and details of your underwater subjects.
- **Composition**: Experiment with different angles and perspectives to create dynamic and visually interesting shots.
- **Patience**: Be prepared to spend time waiting for the perfect shot, as underwater photography can be challenging but ultimately rewarding.

### Bubble Planet Exhibit
- **Research**: Familiarize yourself with the different species of marine life on display at the Bubble Planet exhibit to better capture their unique characteristics.
- **Timing**: Visit the exhibit during peak hours for the best lighting conditions and to avoid crowds.
- **Focus**: Pay attention to the details and textures of the bubbles and marine life to create striking and memorable images.
- **Experiment**: Try different camera settings and techniques to capture the essence of the exhibit in a creative and engaging way.

### Razor Clam Digging
- **Gear**: Make sure you have the right tools, such as a clam gun or shovel, to dig for razor clams effectively.
- **Location**: Research the best beaches for razor clam digging and plan your trip accordingly.
- **Timing**: Check the tide schedule and go digging during low tide for the best chance of finding clams.
- **Safety**: Be mindful of the environment and follow local regulations to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.
