# ARRI’s Customizable Camera Control App Exits BETA, Now Available for Subscription

In the world of photography and filmmaking, having precise control over your camera settings is crucial for achieving the perfect shot. ARRI, a leading manufacturer of professional camera equipment, has recently announced the official launch of their customizable camera control app, which has now exited the BETA phase and is available for subscription. This app allows photographers and filmmakers to have complete control over their ARRI cameras right at their fingertips, making it easier than ever to capture stunning images and footage.

## Introduction to ARRI’s Camera Control App

ARRI’s camera control app is designed to work seamlessly with ARRI’s line of professional digital cinema cameras, including the popular ALEXA Mini LF and ALEXA SXT models. The app allows users to remotely control all aspects of their camera settings, including exposure, focus, white balance, and more. With the ability to customize the app’s interface to suit their specific needs, photographers and filmmakers can quickly access the tools they need to capture the perfect shot.

### 2. Use the Remote Control Functionality
The app’s remote control functionality allows you to control your camera from a distance, making it perfect for shooting in hard-to-reach or dangerous locations. Take advantage of this feature to capture unique angles and perspectives.

### 3. Experiment with Different Camera Settings
Don’t be afraid to experiment with different camera settings using the app. Try adjusting the exposure, white balance, and focus settings to see how they affect the final image. This hands-on approach will help you develop your skills as a photographer or filmmaker.

### 4. Stay Up to Date with Software Updates
To ensure that you are getting the most out of ARRI’s camera control app, make sure to stay up to date with the latest software updates. These updates may introduce new features and improvements that can enhance your workflow and image quality.

With ARRI’s customizable camera control app now available for subscription, photographers and filmmakers have access to a powerful tool that can help them take their work to the next level.
