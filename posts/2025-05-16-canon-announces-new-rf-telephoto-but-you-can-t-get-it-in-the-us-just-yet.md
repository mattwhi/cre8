# Canon Announces New RF Telephoto, But You Can’t Get It in the US Just Yet

## Introduction to the RF 800mm f/11 IS STM and RF 600mm f/11 IS STM

The RF 800mm f/11 IS STM and RF 600mm f/11 IS STM are both part of Canon's new super-telephoto lens lineup for their RF mount cameras. These lenses are designed to provide photographers with a lightweight and portable option for capturing distant subjects without sacrificing image quality. The f/11 aperture may seem limiting to some, but with today's high ISO capabilities, these lenses are more than capable of producing stunning images in a variety of shooting conditions.

## Practical Tips for Getting Your Hands on These Lenses

### 2. Use a Mail Forwarding Service

If you find a retailer that is willing to ship the RF 800mm f/11 IS STM or RF 600mm f/11 IS STM to the US, you can use a mail forwarding service to have the lenses shipped to your address. Services like Shipito or MyUS allow you to have packages shipped to a local address in the country of purchase, and then forwarded on to your US address for a fee.

### 3. Keep an Eye on Canon's US Website

While the RF 800mm f/11 IS STM and RF 600mm f/11 IS STM are not currently available in the US, Canon may announce their availability in the near future. Be sure to keep an eye on Canon's US website and social media channels for any updates on when these lenses will be officially released in the US.

## Conclusion

Although the Canon RF 800mm f/11 IS STM and RF 600mm f/11 IS STM are not yet available in the US, there are ways to get your hands on these exciting new lenses if you're willing to put in a little extra effort.
