# Rebranded Capture One Adds Features to Keep Photographers Inside the App

Capture One, the popular photo editing software, has recently undergone a rebranding and introduced new features designed to keep photographers inside the app for all their editing needs. With the release of Capture One 22, the company aims to provide a seamless editing experience that streamlines the workflow for photographers of all skill levels. In this blog post, we will explore the new features and improvements in Capture One 22 and how they can benefit photographers.

## Introduction to Capture One 22

Capture One has long been a favorite among professional photographers for its powerful editing capabilities and intuitive user interface. With the release of Capture One 22, the software has been rebranded with a sleek new look and improved performance. The new version includes a range of new features and improvements that make editing photos easier and more efficient.

One of the standout features of Capture One 22 is the new Speed Edit tool, which allows users to quickly make adjustments to their photos using keyboard shortcuts. This feature is perfect for photographers who need to make quick edits on the go without having to navigate through multiple menus. The Speed Edit tool makes it easy to adjust exposure, contrast, and color with just a few keystrokes.

## New Features in Capture One 22

### Speed Edit Tool

The Speed Edit tool in Capture One 22 is a game-changer for photographers who want to streamline their editing workflow. With this feature, users can make adjustments to their photos quickly and easily using keyboard shortcuts. This allows for a more efficient editing process, saving time and improving productivity.

### Improved Color Grading

Capture One 22 also includes improvements to the color grading tools, making it easier than ever to adjust the colors in your photos. The new Color Balance tool allows users to make precise adjustments to the shadows, midtones, and highlights of their photos, giving them more control over the overall look and feel of their images.

### Enhanced Export Options

In addition to the new editing features, Capture One 22 also includes enhanced export options that make it easier to share your photos with clients and on social media. The software now includes presets for popular social media platforms, making it easy to export your photos in the correct format and resolution for each platform.
