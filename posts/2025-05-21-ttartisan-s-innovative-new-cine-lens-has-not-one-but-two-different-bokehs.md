# TTArtisan’s Innovative New Cine Lens Has Not One, But Two Different Bokehs

As a photographer, one of the most important elements of a photo is the bokeh effect. Bokeh refers to the aesthetic quality of the out-of-focus areas in an image, and it can truly make or break a photo. TTArtisan, a well-known lens manufacturer, has recently released an innovative new cine lens that takes bokeh to a whole new level. This new lens not only offers one type of bokeh, but two, giving photographers even more creative possibilities when capturing stunning images.

## Introducing TTArtisan’s Dual Bokeh Cine Lens

TTArtisan’s new cine lens is a game-changer for photographers looking to elevate their work to the next level. This lens features two different bokeh effects that can be easily switched between, allowing photographers to experiment and create unique and eye-catching images. The first bokeh effect is a classic circular bokeh, perfect for portraits and close-up shots. The second bokeh effect is a more angular, geometric bokeh that adds a modern and artistic touch to photos.

## Practical Tips for Using Dual Bokeh Effect

When using TTArtisan’s dual bokeh cine lens, there are a few practical tips to keep in mind to make the most out of this innovative feature:

1. **Experiment with Different Subjects**: Try using the circular bokeh effect for portraits and the geometric bokeh effect for landscapes or architectural shots. This will help you showcase the versatility of the lens and create diverse and dynamic images.

2. **Adjust Aperture Settings**: Play around with the aperture settings to control the intensity of the bokeh effect. A wider aperture will create a more pronounced bokeh, while a smaller aperture will create a more subtle effect. Experiment with different settings to find the perfect balance for your desired look.

3. **Focus on Composition**: Keep in mind that the bokeh effect can enhance the overall composition of your image. Use it to draw attention to your main subject or create a sense of depth and dimension in your photos. Be mindful of how the bokeh interacts with the rest of the elements in your frame to create a cohesive and visually appealing image.

4. **Practice and Experiment**: Don’t be afraid to push the boundaries and try new things with TTArtisan’s dual bokeh cine lens. Take the time to practice and experiment with different shooting scenarios and subjects to fully explore the creative potential of this innovative lens. The more you play around with the dual bokeh effects, the more unique and captivating your photos will be.
