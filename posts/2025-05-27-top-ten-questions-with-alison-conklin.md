# Top Ten Questions With Alison Conklin

Alison Conklin is a talented photographer known for her stunning wedding and portrait photography. With years of experience in the industry, Alison has a wealth of knowledge to share with aspiring photographers. In this blog post, we sit down with Alison to ask her the top ten questions that photographers often have. From tips on lighting to advice on building a successful photography business, Alison shares her insights and expertise.

## 1. How did you get started in photography?

Alison: I have always had a passion for capturing special moments and emotions through photography. I started taking photos as a hobby and eventually decided to turn my passion into a career. I took courses, practiced constantly, and worked with other photographers to learn as much as I could.

## 2. What is your favorite type of photography to shoot?

Alison: I love shooting weddings and portraits because I get to capture the joy and love that people share on their special days. There is something magical about capturing those moments that will be treasured for a lifetime.

![Alison Conklin](/path/to/image)

## 3. What advice do you have for photographers looking to improve their lighting skills?

Alison: Lighting is crucial in photography, so it's essential to understand how to use natural light and artificial light sources effectively. Practice with different lighting setups and experiment with different techniques to see what works best for you. Don't be afraid to play around with shadows and highlights to create depth and dimension in your photos.

## 4. How do you approach posing your subjects for a photoshoot?

Alison: I believe in capturing authentic moments, so I prefer to keep posing natural and relaxed. I like to give my subjects some guidance and direction while allowing them to be themselves. It's essential to make your subjects feel comfortable and at ease to capture genuine emotions and expressions.

## 5. What equipment do you recommend for photographers just starting?

Alison: Starting with a good camera body and a versatile lens is essential. Invest in quality equipment that will grow with you as you improve your skills. A tripod, external flash, and reflectors are also handy tools to have in your kit.

## 6. How do you market yourself as a photographer?

Alison: Building a strong online presence is crucial for marketing yourself as a photographer. Create a professional website and social media profiles to showcase your work and attract potential clients. Networking with other professionals in the industry and collaborating on projects can also help you gain exposure.

## 7. What is your editing process like?

Alison: I believe in keeping my editing process simple and natural. I use editing software to enhance colors, contrast, and clarity while maintaining the integrity of the original image. I aim to create timeless photos that will stand the test of time.

## 8. How do you stay inspired and creative in your work?

Alison: I find inspiration in everyday moments, nature, art, and music. I also draw inspiration from other photographers and artists whose work I admire. It's essential to take breaks, recharge, and explore new ideas to stay inspired and creative in your work.

## 9. What advice do you have for photographers looking to start their own business?

Alison: Starting a photography business takes hard work, dedication, and perseverance. Create a solid business plan, establish your brand, and define your target market. Offer exceptional customer service, deliver quality work, and always strive to exceed your clients' expectations.

## 10. What is the most rewarding part of being a photographer?

Alison: The most rewarding part of being a photographer is capturing moments that evoke emotions and memories for my clients. Knowing that my work brings joy and happiness to others is incredibly fulfilling. I feel grateful to do what I love and create art that resonates with people.

In conclusion, Alison Conklin's insights and advice offer valuable guidance for photographers looking to improve their skills and build a successful career in photography. From mastering lighting techniques to marketing yourself effectively, Alison's expertise shines through in her work and words. Remember to stay true to your artistic vision, keep learning and growing, and above all, have fun capturing the beauty of life through your lens.
