# Lightroom Tutorial: How to Make One Color Pop in a Black & White Photo

Are you looking to add a creative touch to your black and white photography? One popular technique is to make one color stand out in an otherwise monochromatic image. This can create a stunning visual impact and draw the viewer's attention to a specific element in the photo. In this tutorial, we will show you how to achieve this effect using Adobe Lightroom.

## Introduction to Making Colors Pop in Black & White Photography

Black and white photography has a timeless and classic appeal, but adding a pop of color can inject a modern twist into your images. By isolating a single color and desaturating the rest of the photo, you can create a striking contrast that highlights the chosen hue.

This technique is commonly used in fashion photography, portraiture, and landscape photography to draw attention to specific elements such as a vibrant red lipstick, a colorful flower, or a striking sunset. With the right tools and a bit of practice, you can easily achieve this effect in Lightroom.

## Practical Tips for Making Colors Pop in Black & White Photos

### Step 1: Import Your Black & White Photo into Lightroom

Start by importing your black and white photo into Lightroom. If you haven't converted your image to black and white yet, you can do so by selecting the Black & White option in the Basic panel of the Develop module.

### Step 2: Select the HSL/Color Panel

Navigate to the HSL/Color panel in the Develop module. Here, you will find sliders for adjusting the Hue, Saturation, and Luminance of individual colors in your photo.

### Step 3: Identify the Color You Want to Make Pop

Take a close look at your photo and identify the color you want to make stand out. This could be a bold red, a vibrant green, or any other hue that catches your eye.

### Step 4: Increase the Saturation of the Chosen Color

Using the Saturation slider in the HSL/Color panel, increase the saturation of the color you want to make pop. You will see the selected hue become more vibrant and pronounced in the black and white image.

### Step 5: Fine-Tune the Hue and Luminance

If necessary, adjust the Hue and Luminance sliders to further enhance the selected color. The Hue slider allows you to shift the color towards a different hue, while the Luminance slider controls the brightness of the color.

### Step 6: Refine Your Selection with the Adjustment Brush

For more precise control, you can use the Adjustment Brush tool to paint over the area containing the color you want to emphasize. This will allow you to make targeted adjustments to the saturation, hue, and luminance of that specific area.

### Step 7: Final Touches and Export

Once you are satisfied with the results, make any final adjustments to the overall image and then export your photo. You now have a striking black and white image with a pop of color that commands attention.

## Conclusion

Adding a pop of color to a black and white photo can elevate your photography and create a visually compelling image. With the tools and techniques available in Lightroom, you can easily achieve this effect and make your photos stand out. Experiment with different colors and subjects to find the perfect balance and create stunning black and white images that grab the viewer's attention.

Next time you're editing a black and white photo in Lightroom, don't be afraid to play around with color pops and see how they can enhance your images.
