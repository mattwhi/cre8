# Pentax M 28mm 2.8 – My Perfect Wide Lens

If you're a photographer looking to add a versatile wide-angle lens to your kit, the Pentax M 28mm 2.8 is a fantastic option to consider. With its compact size, excellent image quality, and affordable price point, this lens has become a favorite among many photographers, myself included.

## Introduction

The Pentax M 28mm 2.8 is a manual focus lens that was originally released in the 1980s. Despite its age, this lens has stood the test of time and continues to be a popular choice for photographers looking for a wide-angle option. Its 28mm focal length makes it ideal for landscapes, architecture, street photography, and more.

## Practical Tips

Here are some practical tips for using the Pentax M 28mm 2.8 to get the best results:

1. **Focus Carefully**: Since this lens is manual focus, it's important to take your time when focusing to ensure that your subject is sharp. Use the focus peaking feature on your camera if available to assist with achieving accurate focus.

2. **Use a Tripod**: When shooting landscapes or in low light conditions, using a tripod can help you achieve sharp images with this lens. The 28mm focal length is wide enough to capture a lot of detail, so stability is key.

3. **Experiment with Composition**: The wide-angle perspective of the Pentax M 28mm 2.8 allows you to get creative with your compositions. Try shooting from different angles and perspectives to see what works best for your subject.

4. **Stop Down for Sharpness**: While this lens performs well wide open at f/2.8, stopping down to around f/5.6-f/8 will give you optimal sharpness across the frame.

5. **Shoot in RAW**: Shooting in RAW format will give you more flexibility when it comes to editing your images later. This will allow you to make adjustments to exposure, white balance, and more without losing image quality.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, the Pentax M 28mm 2.8 is a fantastic wide-angle lens that offers excellent image quality and versatility at an affordable price point. Whether you're a seasoned photographer looking to expand your kit or a beginner looking for a quality wide lens, the Pentax M 28mm 2.8 is a great choice. With a bit of practice and experimentation, you can capture stunning images with this lens that will take your photography to the next level.
